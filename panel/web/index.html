IMEI

advanced · modifies modem
Changing the IMEI rewrites the modem's identifier and is not reversible to the factory default — the previous value has to be typed back in by hand. Some carriers restrict SA to whitelisted devices; spoofing to one they know unlocks SA there, but may break the SIM contract or violate the ToS depending on the country. Use at your own risk.
